# ChayilResources — Teacher Resource Platform

> **Find the right resource for the right class, subject and topic — quickly.**

ChayilResources is a curriculum-aligned digital resource library and lesson-preparation companion for teachers across **Primary, JHS, SHS and TVET** education.

Our mission: make high-quality teaching and learning resources easily accessible to every teacher, so they spend less time searching and more time preparing effective learning experiences.

## Vision

To make high-quality teaching and learning resources easily accessible to every teacher.

Unlike a conventional digital library or textbook platform that answers *"Where can I read this book?"*, ChayilResources answers:

> *"I am teaching this class and this topic. What do I need to teach it well?"*

**Long-term vision:** an education resource ecosystem connecting **Teachers ↔ Resources ↔ Curriculum ↔ Publishers ↔ Education Organizations ↔ Professional Learning**, supporting the full teaching cycle: **Plan → Prepare → Teach → Assess → Reflect → Improve**.

## Who It's For

**Primary users:** Teachers in:
- Primary (lower and upper)
- Junior High School (JHS)
- Senior High School (SHS)
- TVET / vocational and technical instructors

**Secondary users:**
school leaders, curriculum leaders, teacher trainers, education organizations, NGOs, resource authors, publishers, and approved education partners.

The teacher remains the central user.

## Core Principles

1. **Teacher-first** — Does this make a teacher's work easier?
2. **Curriculum-aligned** — Organized around the curriculum, not just files.
3. **Easy discovery** — Search or browse, fast.
4. **Quality and trust** — Reviewed, classified, clearly identified.
5. **Practical classroom use** — Resources you can actually teach with.
6. **Inclusive access** — Works for varied connectivity, devices, and digital experience.

## What Teachers Get

### Resource Library
Core resources:
- Digital textbooks, teacher guides, lesson plans, schemes of learning
- Worksheets, quizzes, assessment questions, exam preparation
- Classroom activities, teaching strategies, revision materials

Supporting resources:
- Videos, diagrams, posters, presentations, reading materials
- Practical activities, project ideas, remedial and enrichment resources

Every resource includes: title, education level, class/year, subject, strand/topic, resource type, curriculum alignment, description, author/publisher, edition/date, and review status.

### Three Ways to Discover
1. **Search** — e.g. "JHS 2 fractions lesson plan", "Primary 5 reading comprehension", "SHS Biology photosynthesis"
2. **Browse by Curriculum** — Level → Class → Subject → Strand → Sub-strand → Topic → Resources
3. **Browse by Subject/Class** — Level → Class → Subject → Topic (simpler route)

### Personalized Teacher Dashboard
- My Subjects / My Classes
- Continue Learning, Recently Added, Recommended for You
- Saved Resources, My Downloads

### Save, Organize & Use Offline
- Create collections (e.g. "JHS 1 English", "BECE Preparation", "Term 1 Resources")
- Preview before downloading: description, subject, class, topic, type, pages, author, alignment, review status
- Download / save for offline use — built for low / unreliable connectivity

### Quality, Feedback & Contributions
- Quality badges: `Reviewed` · `Curriculum-Aligned` · `Contributor Resource` · `Recommended`
- Teacher feedback: useful / not useful, appropriate, needs improvement, incorrect, outdated, report a problem
- Future contributions: teachers, publishers, NGOs and partners can submit resources via a review process

Plus: Assessment Resource Centre, lesson preparation packs, teaching strategies (What → Why → How → Example), professional learning, and purposeful notifications.

## Content Standards

All resources are evaluated for curriculum relevance, accuracy, age-appropriateness, clarity, usefulness, source/authorship, copyright permission, and currency. Only licensed, original, open-licensed, or otherwise legitimately distributable materials are included, with clear copyright holder / author / publisher / permitted use attribution.
